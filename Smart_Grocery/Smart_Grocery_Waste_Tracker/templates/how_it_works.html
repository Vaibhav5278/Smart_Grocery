🚀 How Our Application Works

Our Smart Grocery Waste Management System helps businesses track inventory, minimize waste, and maximize savings. Here’s a simple step-by-step guide:

Step 1: User Registration & Login

Business owners register or login to create a personalized account. Authentication ensures secure access to the system and their own data.

Step 2: Add & Manage Grocery Items

Users add grocery items with details like name, category, purchase date, expiry date, supplier, price, and MRP. Items are stored in the GroceryItem model and linked to the logged-in user.

Step 3: Expiry Alerts & Monitoring

The system automatically checks expiry dates. Items expiring soon are highlighted, and expired items are moved to the WastedGroceryItem log.

Step 4: Waste Logging

Expired, damaged, or manually discarded items are logged with details like quantity, reason, and waste date. This ensures waste is tracked instead of just deleting items.

Step 5: Reports & Insights

The system generates interactive charts using Plotly:

  • 📊 Pie Chart → Waste by Reason
  • 📈 Line Chart → Daily Waste Trends
  • 📉 Bar Chart → Wasted Items Overview

Step 6: Savings & Profit Tracking

Users can calculate profits vs. losses by comparing sold items, wasted items, and remaining stock. The system shows if the business is in profit or loss with real-time values.

Step 7: User & Profile Management

Each user has their own Profile (store name, data tracking). All data is personalized and securely connected with their account.
