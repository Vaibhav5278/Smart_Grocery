Track. Save. Sustain.

Empowering both shops and homes to fight grocery waste.

🌍 The Problem

Whether it's leftover stock in stores or forgotten items in homes, food waste is a global issue. Poor tracking and over-purchasing lead to massive waste — hurting both the environment and your wallet.

💡 Our Solution

Our app helps both store owners and households manage grocery inventories smartly. Track expiry dates, log waste daily, and visualize your waste patterns to take action early.

🎯 Our Goals

  • Reduce waste from shelves, pantries, and storerooms
  • Optimize stock rotation in retail and kitchen settings
  • Contribute to environmental sustainability
  • Save costs through smart inventory control
